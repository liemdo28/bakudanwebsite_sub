Bandera Road Saké Happy Hour: What's Different at Our Original Saké Bar

If you've been to Bakudan's Stone Oak or La Cantera locations for happy hour, Bandera Road runs differently on purpose. Instead of a cocktail list, Bandera is built around a dedicated saké bar — it's the one location where that's the whole point, not an add-on.

What's Actually on the Menu

Bandera's happy hour, daily from 3–6 PM, centers on saké cocktails at $5 and saké sangria pitchers at $20. That's the core of it — there isn't a parallel cocktail list running alongside it the way there is at Stone Oak and La Cantera.

Why It's Set Up This Way

Bandera was Bakudan's third location, opening in February 2023, and it's the only one of the three with a dedicated saké bar instead of a full cocktail program. If you're specifically after saké — not just a drink to go with dinner, but saké as the point of the visit — Bandera is where that's built into the experience rather than being one option among many.

Same Ramen, Different Bar

Nothing about the food changes here. The full ramen and starters menu is the same as Stone Oak and La Cantera — Garlic Tonkotsu, Spicy Umami Miso, Vegetarian, and everything else. It's specifically the drink program that's different, and specifically during that 3–6 PM window that it matters.

Planning a Visit

Bandera Road is at 11309 Bandera Rd Ste 111. If saké sangria pitchers sound like the move for a group, showing up inside the 3–6 PM window is when that pricing applies — outside of it, you're ordering off the regular drink menu.

A Local's FAQ: The Most-Asked Questions About Bakudan Ramen

These are the questions that come up most often about visiting Bakudan Ramen — locations, hours, ordering, and the menu. Everything below is answered directly, without padding.

A Note on What's Not Covered Here

This FAQ sticks to what's verified and current: locations, hours, ordering, and menu basics. If you have a question about something more specific — gift cards, catering, or dietary restrictions beyond what's listed on the vegetarian and kids' menu items — the most reliable answer is to call your nearest location directly, since that's not something covered in general marketing content.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many Bakudan Ramen locations are there?

Three, all in San Antonio: Bandera Road, Stone Oak, and La Cantera.

What are Bakudan Ramen's hours?

Monday–Thursday 11:00 AM–8:30 PM, Friday–Saturday 11:00 AM–9:00 PM, and Sunday 11:00 AM–8:00 PM, the same at all three locations.

Is the menu the same at every location?

The ramen, starters, and food menu are the same everywhere. What differs is the drink program: Stone Oak and La Cantera have a full cocktail and wine list, while Bandera has a dedicated saké bar instead.

When is happy hour?

Daily from 3–6 PM at all three locations, though what's included varies — Stone Oak and La Cantera run cocktail-focused happy hour pricing, while Bandera's is saké-focused.

How do I order online?

Through the order page, which routes you to your chosen location's ordering system on Toast for pickup or delivery. Delivery areas, fees, and estimated times are managed by Toast.

Is there a vegetarian option?

Yes — the Vegetarian ramen bowl, built on an oat milk and garlic oil base with tofu, spinach, and cabbage, with no animal broth.

Is there a kids' menu?

Yes — Kids Ramen is $8.50, a simplified chicken broth bowl with chicken chashu, corn, and thin noodles.

Which location opened first?

La Cantera, in May 2019. Stone Oak followed in October 2022, and Bandera Road opened in February 2023.

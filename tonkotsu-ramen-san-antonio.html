Tonkotsu Ramen in San Antonio

Rich, creamy tonkotsu broth simmered for 6-7 hours every morning.

Bakudan Ramen is San Antonio's destination for authentic tonkotsu ramen. Our tonkotsu broth is slow-simmered for 6-7 hours every morning, creating the rich, creamy, deeply savory base that defines great tonkotsu.

Our Garlic Tonkotsu features house-made tonkotsu broth, tender pork chashu, kikurage mushroom, crispy garlic chips, seasoned ajitama egg, and nori. Come taste why San Antonio loves our tonkotsu.

FAQ

What is tonkotsu ramen?

Tonkotsu ramen features a rich, creamy pork bone broth simmered for hours. Bakudan simmers ours for 6-7 hours daily.

Where can I get tonkotsu ramen in San Antonio?

At any of our three locations: Bandera, Stone Oak, and La Cantera.

How long does Bakudan simmer its tonkotsu broth?

Our tonkotsu broth is simmered for 6-7 hours every morning for maximum richness.

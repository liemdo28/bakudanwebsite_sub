Visiting San Antonio? Finding Bakudan Near Six Flags Fiesta Texas & The Rim

If you're visiting San Antonio and staying anywhere near Six Flags Fiesta Texas or The Rim shopping center on the northwest side, Bakudan's La Cantera location — 17619 La Cantera Pkwy Unit 208 — is in the same general area, off La Cantera Parkway near the Shops at La Cantera and the Palladium IMAX.

What's Actually Nearby

La Cantera Parkway sits close to the Shops at La Cantera and the Palladium IMAX theater, both real, well-known parts of that northwest San Antonio shopping and entertainment area. If your trip already has you in that part of town for the theme park or the shops, this location doesn't require a separate detour.

A Practical Stop, Not a Detour

This is Bakudan's original location, open since May 2019, and it runs the same full ramen menu as the other two locations, plus a full cocktail and wine program. If you're looking for a real sit-down meal after a day at the park or the mall rather than another fast-food stop, it's a straightforward option in the immediate area.

Hours for Trip Planning

La Cantera is open 11:00 AM–8:30 PM Monday through Thursday, 11:00 AM–9:00 PM Friday and Saturday, and 11:00 AM–8:00 PM Sunday — worth checking against your park or shopping hours if you're trying to fit a meal in around a set schedule.

If You're New to Bakudan Entirely

If this is your first time at Bakudan and not just your first time at this location, the menu runs from milder chicken-based bowls like Bakudan Chicken to richer options like Garlic Tonkotsu and spicier ones like Spicy Umami Miso — there's a reasonable range whether you're traveling with picky eaters or looking for something bold after a day out.

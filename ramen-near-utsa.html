Ramen Near UTSA

Bakudan Ramen Bandera is minutes from UTSA. Authentic Japanese ramen on San Antonio northwest side.

Looking for ramen near UTSA? Bakudan Ramen Bandera is located just minutes from the University of Texas at San Antonio (UTSA) main campus, in the shopping center at Bandera Road and Loop 1604.

Our Bandera location serves the full Bakudan menu: Garlic Tonkotsu, Spicy Umami Miso, Cilantro Lime Chicken, and Vegetarian Shoyu. Plenty of parking, walk-ins welcome. Order online for pickup or delivery.
